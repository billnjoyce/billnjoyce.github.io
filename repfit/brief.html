Editor brief for App Store Editorial — updated October 2026

Two apps,
one gym floor

One for the person lifting. One for the person coaching them. Both written by one developer in Seoul, in Korean and English from the first line of code.

60kg × 12→top of rep range→+2.5kg RepFit.ai shows the lifter why the weight is the weight — in their own logged sets.

base+commission−clawback=take-home RepFit Coach tells the trainer what actually lands in their account this month.

RepFit.ai

For the lifter · version 1.3

  • Why this weight. Tap the prescribed load and the chain unfolds: last set, verdict, decision. No language model — the logic is deterministic double progression, which is exactly why it can be shown.
  • One-tap override. Good / Normal / Rough. Sleep and stress move strength and no log can see them, so the coach adjusts instead of arguing.
  • Rest on the Lock Screen. A Live Activity carries the countdown and the next set's numbers, so the phone stays in a pocket between sets. Workouts write to Apple Health.

RepFit Coach

For the trainer · version 1.4

  • The payslip, before it arrives. Enter each gym's deal as the contract reads — base, rate, incentive ladder — and see take-home broken into its three parts.
  • Counted in sessions, not days. Korean PT sells in 10, 20 and 50-session packages, and the renewal conversation happens at session 7 of 10. The engine was rebuilt around that.
  • Nothing on the Home Screen that shouldn't be. The widget shows classes left and take-home, and deliberately no member names — a trainer's phone is visible to whoever stands beside them.

Things an editor may want to check

Accessibility
Rebuilt in these releases. Every hard-coded font size scales with Dynamic Type through UIFontMetrics; icon-only controls, the workout heatmap and the charts carry VoiceOver labels; colour-only status indicators were replaced with symbols; Reduce Motion is honoured. Accessibility Nutrition Labels are declared in App Store Connect.
Localization
Korean and English throughout, written together rather than translated. RepFit Coach models a market structure English-language trainer apps do not: session packages and gym commission ladders.
Platform adoption
ActivityKit (rest timer), WidgetKit (Home Screen and Lock Screen), HealthKit (write-only — the apps never request read access), App Intents for Shortcuts and Siri, Swift Charts, StoreKit 2.
Privacy
Both apps work fully without an account. Health data is written, never read, never leaves the device, and is never used for advertising. The widget App Group carries a handful of numbers and no personal identifiers.
Made by
One person, in Seoul. Design, engineering, copy and both localizations.
