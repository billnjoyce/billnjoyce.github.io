Privacy Policy

Sleep Frequency · Joyce Studios · Effective November 6, 2026

1. Overview

Joyce Studios (the “Company”) provides the Sleep Frequency iOS app (the “Service”) and takes care to handle your information responsibly and in line with applicable law. This policy explains what information we process and why. The Service works without registration or login and does not ask for your name, phone number or email address. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library.

2. Information we collect and why

• Subscription purchase information (the transaction ID, original transaction ID and signed transaction data issued by the App Store, product ID, subscription status and expiry date): to verify your Premium entitlement and reflect renewals, cancellations and refunds. We never receive your card or other payment details; Apple processes payment. • Anonymous identifiers (a random ID created by Firebase anonymous authentication and a random per-install ID): to tie your entitlement to the device and to count anonymous usage by app version and platform. They are not linked to your name or email. • Anonymous usage statistics (app version, platform (iOS), installs, updates, daily use, purchase occurrences and the names of a few feature events): to improve and stabilize the Service. • Invitation information (invitation code, invitation and reward history): to grant friend-invitation rewards. • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging): to send announcements and notifications if you allow notifications. • Advertising information (advertising identifier (IDFA), ad interactions, device information and the IP address used to estimate approximate location): processed by Google AdMob to show ads to free users. The advertising identifier is used only if you choose “Allow” in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. • Information kept only on your device: your selected sounds, mixes, settings, sleep records, streaks, gem collection and bedtime reminder time are not sent to our servers and are deleted when you delete the app.

3. App permissions and system features

• Notifications (optional): bedtime reminders and announcements. All core features work if you decline, and you can change this any time in device Settings. • Tracking permission request (App Tracking Transparency, optional): personalizes ads shown to free users. Every feature works the same if you decline (ads may still appear, without personalization). • Background audio: used only to keep sound you started playing going while the screen is off, and not used when nothing is playing. The lock-screen player display can be turned off in Settings. • Haptics: touch feedback; can be turned off in Settings. The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data.

4. Retention

We delete information without undue delay once its purpose has been served. Subscription records on the server are kept while the subscription is active and for up to one year after it ends for fraud prevention and dispute handling, then deleted. Information we must keep by law is kept for the required period. Information stored on your device is deleted when you delete the app.

5. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your information and do not provide it to third parties without consent except where the law requires. The following providers process information to operate the Service: • Apple Inc.: App Store payments and subscriptions, in-app rating request • Google LLC: Firebase (anonymous authentication, database, server functions, cloud messaging), Google AdMob advertising and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent message Each provider’s own privacy policy also applies to its processing.

6. International transfers

Information may be transferred to and processed on servers outside your country through Apple and Google services. The subscription verification server runs in the Seoul region (asia-northeast3). The information transferred is the information in Section 2, the timing and method are network communication when you use the Service, and the recipients are Apple Inc. and Google LLC.

7. Advertising and tracking

Free users see Google AdMob ads. When you first open the app (or as local law requires) you are asked whether to allow tracking and for ad consent; the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized ads only if you allow it. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. No ads are shown while you have an active Premium subscription. We do not sell or provide information to data brokers in order to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of processing of your information at any time. To delete information stored on our servers, email ibillkim@gmail.com (the scope of deletion for subscription records depends on subscription status). Information inside the app is erased by deleting the app, and notifications and tracking permission can be changed in device Settings.

9. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards: server-side values such as entitlements are encrypted and sealed against tampering, purchase information is stored encrypted, only the server can grant entitlements, and request rates are limited. All communication is encrypted with TLS.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 14 (or the age set by local law), and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child’s information has been collected, contact us below.

11. Legal bases and regional rights

Where the law requires a legal basis, we process information for performance of a contract (verifying and providing your subscription), legitimate interests (service security, fraud prevention, bug fixing, anonymous statistics), your consent (tracking permission, notifications) and legal obligations. • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR): you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, may withdraw consent at any time and may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. • Brazil (LGPD): you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, deletion, portability and information about sharing. • United States (including California CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell personal information and do not directly share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. For free users Google AdMob may serve ads under its own settings, which you control through the app’s tracking permission. You may exercise your rights without discrimination.

12. Data at a glance

• Purchase transaction information, product ID, subscription status and expiry: subscription verification · stored on our server (Seoul region) · while subscribed and up to 1 year after • Anonymous identifiers (Firebase anonymous ID, per-install random ID): entitlement linking and anonymous statistics · stored on our server · same as above • Anonymous usage statistics: service improvement · stored on our server · for the reporting period • Invitation code and reward history: invitation rewards · stored on our server · reward period and up to 1 year after • Push notification token: sending notifications · stored in Firebase · until the app is deleted or the token refreshes • Advertising identifier and ad interactions: ads for free users · processed by Google AdMob under its own policy • Sounds, mixes, settings, sleep records, streaks, reminder time: used only on your device · deleted when you delete the app

13. Identifiers and SDKs

• The app uses Firebase anonymous authentication, which creates a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email or phone number. • The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK and Firebase SDKs. Google may process the advertising identifier, IP address and device, performance and crash diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and keep its SDKs stable. • Premium subscribers see no AdMob ads. • The app does not use separate third-party analytics tools that profile you across other apps and websites.

14. Your choices

• Tracking permission and ad personalization: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking (or the in-app ad consent screen) • Notifications and bedtime reminder: app Settings and device notification settings • Touch haptics, sound effects and lock-screen player: app Settings • Subscription: manage or cancel in App Store > Subscriptions. Cancelling does not delete server records; contact us if you want them deleted. • Deletion and access requests: email us below from an address you can access. We may ask for the minimum information needed to locate records (for example an invitation code). We reply within 30 days (sooner where the law requires) and will tell you if we must keep records for legal reasons.

15. Breaches and automated decisions

If a breach likely to harm you occurs, we will notify you and the competent authorities without undue delay as the law requires and explain what happened and what you can do. We do not make decisions with legal effect on you solely by automated processing or profiling. Subscription verification only compares your purchase with App Store records.
