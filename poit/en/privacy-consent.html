POIT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Effective: October 27, 2026

Joyce Studios ("we") provides POIT on iOS, Android, as a Toss mini-app, and on the web. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Browsing without logging in needs no consent from you. Location, notification and ad-tracking permissions are each requested separately by your device.

We do not collect passwords, contacts, photos, microphone, health or financial data.

2. Public information

Public notes (text, color, hashtags, location on the globe and nickname) are visible to other users and to anyone with a share link. Anonymous notes hide your nickname. Private notes are visible only to you.

3. Location and live sharing

We use location only with your permission. A note's location is stored with the note. Live sharing starts only when you start it, is shown only to your followers, defaults to approximate location, and stops when you end it or it expires; we then delete the live location. We do not sell location data or use it for tracking. You can revoke permission in device settings at any time.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We use these processors:

  • Google LLC — Firebase (authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, FCM push, hosting); Google AdMob (ads, consent)
  • Apple Inc. — Sign in with Apple, APNs, App Tracking Transparency
  • Viva Republica (Toss) — Toss Login and ads (Toss mini-app only)
  • Kakao Corp. — converting coordinates to place names / map display

Data may be processed in countries other than yours (including Singapore, South Korea and the United States). We may disclose data if required by law.

5. Advertising and tracking

Ads are shown by Google AdMob and others. On iOS you control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; if you decline, you are not tracked and ads may be non-personalized. Where required (e.g., EEA/UK) we show a consent form first. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data until you delete your account. Delete in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, profile and all notes are then permanently deleted without undue delay. (Toss users can also disconnect POIT from the Toss app.)
  • Some records may be kept as required by law (e.g., access logs for 3 months in Korea). Copies already shared or captured by others cannot be recalled.

7. Your rights

You may access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, withdraw consent, and (where applicable, e.g., EEA/UK/California) request portability and opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of data — we do not sell data. Contact us at ibillkim@gmail.com; we respond within a reasonable time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

POIT is not directed to children. You must be at least 14 (13 where permitted). We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age and delete it when found. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data.

9. Local storage and cookies

The app and website store a login token, last globe view, onboarding status, active live-sharing session and a random install ID on your device. The website may use Google Analytics. You can clear these by deleting the app or browser data.

10. Security

We use HTTPS, access rules that restrict private data to its owner, no password storage, and least-privilege access. No system is perfectly secure.

11. Changes and contact

We will notify you in the app at least 7 days before material changes take effect (30 days for unfavorable changes). Contact / data protection officer: Jeonghun Kim, Joyce Studios — ibillkim@gmail.com.
